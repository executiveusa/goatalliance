## Why this matters

Plumbing leads are often urgent. A homeowner with water on the floor does not want vague branding. They need to know who handles the problem, where they serve, what happens after hours, and what proof exists that the business can do the work.

## Profile fields to make clear

A strong plumbing profile should include:

- emergency and after-hours status
- drain, leak, water heater, sewer, and fixture services
- service radius and city boundaries
- license and insurance proof
- brand or equipment specialties
- photos of real work where appropriate
- prevention guidance for common issues

## Better content frame

Do not write generic copy like “trusted local plumbing experts.” Explain the situation. Explain what a customer should check first. Explain when to shut off water. Explain what information to have ready before calling.

That kind of content helps people even before they buy. It also gives AI systems clearer context when matching intent to a contractor profile.

## Agent task

Hermes should score every plumbing candidate for emergency clarity, license visibility, after-hours rules, and proof assets. Missing proof becomes the outreach hook.
