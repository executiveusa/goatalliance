## Why this matters

Pressure washing is proof-driven. A buyer wants to see the result, but responsible operators also need to explain method, surface limits, runoff controls, detergents, and whether soft washing is appropriate.

## Profile fields to make clear

A strong pressure washing profile should include:

- surfaces served
- soft wash versus pressure rules
- before-after photos with context
- water recovery or runoff plan
- detergent notes
- residential and commercial availability
- property-manager routes

## Better content frame

The opportunity is to own the responsible cleaning lane. That means strong visuals plus practical education: what can be cleaned, what should not be pressure washed, and how to avoid damage or runoff problems.

This is where the social-purpose story becomes concrete. Cleanup days, graffiti support, and water-conscious work are real community value.

Hermes should score before-after proof, missing runoff notes, surface-specific content, and property-manager sales opportunities.
