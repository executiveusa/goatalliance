## Why this matters

HVAC buying decisions are technical and seasonal. In the Pacific Northwest, heat pumps, indoor air quality, backup heat, and maintenance plans all need clearer explanation than most contractor websites provide.

## Profile fields to make clear

A strong HVAC profile should include:

- heat pump and furnace service categories
- maintenance plan details
- emergency heat policies
- equipment brands served
- rebate or incentive guidance, with disclaimers
- city and neighborhood service boundaries
- photos of installs and service vehicles

## Better content frame

HVAC content should reduce confusion. Explain the difference between emergency repair, replacement planning, maintenance, and efficiency upgrades. Do not pretend every home has the same answer.

The best content gives a homeowner enough context to ask better questions and helps an AI assistant route them to a more relevant provider.
