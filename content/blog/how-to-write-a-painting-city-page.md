## Why this matters

Painting buyers are visual, but the decision is not only visual. Prep work, product choice, surface condition, weather timing, and warranty expectations matter more than most pages explain.

## Profile fields to make clear

A strong painting profile should include:

- interior, exterior, cabinet, and commercial categories
- surface-prep process
- paint systems and brands
- low-VOC options
- lead-safe practices when applicable
- portfolio examples by surface type
- scheduling and weather-window guidance

## Better content frame

Avoid copy that could fit any painter. Explain the work. Show the prep. Name the surfaces. Organize portfolio images around the problem solved, not just the final look.

That helps people evaluate quality and gives AI systems more useful context.
