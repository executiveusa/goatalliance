## Why this matters

Roofing trust depends on proof. Rain, moss, wind, tree cover, gutters, roof age, and material choice all affect the decision. Generic pages leave too many questions unanswered.

## Profile fields to make clear

A strong roofing profile should include:

- roof types served
- leak repair process
- inspection process
- material brands and warranties
- moss and gutter services
- storm-response rules
- before-after and drone proof where available

## Better content frame

Roofing content should help people understand risk before they are in panic mode. A useful page explains warning signs, inspection timing, and when a repair is not enough.

This is especially important in the Pacific Northwest, where weather windows and moisture issues shape the work.

## Agent task

Hermes should create weather-window content, score proof assets, and flag any warranty or insurance claim that needs verification.
