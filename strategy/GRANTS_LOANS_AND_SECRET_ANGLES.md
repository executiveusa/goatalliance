# Grants, Loans, and Revenue Angles

## Stable base revenue

1. AI Visibility Audit: $199–$499 one-time.
2. Verified profile: $99–$249/month.
3. Premium city/category sponsorship: $500–$2,500/month.
4. Done-for-you AI-readable website upgrade: $1,500–$7,500.
5. Data/API access for property managers and community partners: custom.

## Financing lanes

### SBA 7(a)

Use for software buildout, working capital, AI-related expenses, equipment, and operating runway if eligible.

### SBA Microloan

Use for a smaller launch budget under $50,000: cameras, devices, content production, local outreach, software subscriptions, and first operating expenses.

## Grant and partner lanes

- Community beautification projects.
- Water-conscious cleaning education.
- Safe housing and leak-prevention education.
- Low-VOC painting education.
- Weatherization and heat-pump education with local partners.
- Workforce development for small contractors.
- Digital-readiness support for minority, immigrant, and small local contractors.

## Secret angles

### Supply-house sponsors

Plumbing supply stores, HVAC distributors, roofing suppliers, paint stores, and pressure-washing equipment vendors can sponsor useful local guides without controlling rankings.

### Property manager lead desk

Property managers need repeat vendors. Build a paid vendor-readiness feed by city and vertical.

### Community proof badge

Contractors earn a social-purpose badge by contributing time, money, training, or discounted work to approved local projects.

### AI profile completion bounty

Offer free audit scans, then charge only when the business wants the fixed profile package.

### EcoWash lane

Pressure washers can own an environmental responsibility narrative by publishing runoff controls, surface rules, detergent notes, and cleanup-day commitments.
